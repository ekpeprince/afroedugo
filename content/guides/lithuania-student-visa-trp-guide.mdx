## Overview: Navigating the Lithuanian Student Immigration Pathway

Lithuania has emerged as one of the most attractive higher education destinations in Northern Europe, offering internationally accredited English-taught degrees, affordable living costs, and straightforward Schengen mobility.

For non-EU and international students, entering Lithuania involves two primary legal mechanisms administered by the **Migration Department under the Ministry of the Interior of the Republic of Lithuania (MIGRIS)**:

1. **National Visa D (Long-Stay Student Visa)**: Typically granted for up to 1 year for educational purposes.
2. **Temporary Residence Permit (TRP)**: Biometric European residence card issued for the duration of studies (typically 2 years at a time for degree students).

---

## Step 1: Receiving Your University Admission & Mediation Letter

Before initiating any visa or residence permit application, you must secure official unconditional acceptance from a recognized Lithuanian higher education institution (e.g., Vilnius University, Kaunas University of Technology, Vytautas Magnus University, or Vilnius Tech).

Once your tuition deposit is confirmed, your university issues an electronic **Mediation Letter Number (Tarpininkavimo raštas)** directly into the MIGRIS database. 

> **Important:** You do not receive a physical mediation document. The university generates a unique 8-character code that links your personal file directly in the MIGRIS electronic system.

---

## Step 2: Creating Your MIGRIS Electronic Application

All Lithuanian visa and residence permit applications start online at the official portal ([www.migris.lt](https://www.migris.lt)).

1. Register an account using your personal email.
2. Select **"I am coming to study"** -> **"Temporary Residence Permit"** (or National Visa D).
3. Input your university's **Mediation Letter Number**.
4. Upload digital scans of all required documents in PDF format.
5. Pay the state application fee online.

---

## Step 3: Mandatory Document Checklist

Ensure every document meets strict Lithuanian consular standards:

| Document | Specifications & Legalization |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Valid International Passport** | Valid for at least 3 months beyond the intended period of stay, with at least 2 blank pages. |
| **Mediation Letter Number** | Electronic reference code issued by your Lithuanian university. |
| **Proof of Sufficient Financial Means** | Bank statement showing at least **€5,520** (12 months living expenses) plus travel return funds (~€800). Must be an active international account in your name or with an apostilled sponsorship affidavit. |
| **Certificate of Criminal Clearance (Police Report)** | Issued within the last 6 months by national authorities of countries lived in for the past 2 years. Must have an **Apostille** or consular legalization and certified translation into Lithuanian or English. |
| **Health Insurance Policy** | Valid in all Schengen states with minimum coverage of **€30,000**, covering medical repatriation and emergency care. |
| **Proof of Accommodation** | University dormitory booking voucher, apartment lease agreement, or landlord declaration of address. |

---

## Step 4: Booking Biometrics via External Service Provider (VFS Global)

If there is no Lithuanian Embassy in your home country, Lithuania partners with external visa service providers (such as VFS Global) to collect biometrics and verify original documentation.

- Book your biometric appointment well in advance (ideally 8 to 12 weeks prior to semester start).
- Bring originals and apostilled copies of all transcripts, police clearance, and bank statements.
- Your biometric data (fingerprints and facial photograph) will be captured and transmitted directly to Vilnius for assessment.

---

## Step 5: Decision Timeline & Card Collection

- **Standard Processing**: Up to 60 calendar days.
- **Urgent Processing**: Up to 30 calendar days (additional state fee applies).

Once approved, you will receive an official notification via your MIGRIS portal. If you applied for a TRP from abroad, you will receive your physical residence permit card through the external service provider before departure or receive a National Visa D to enter Lithuania and collect your physical TRP card at a local MIGRIS branch in Vilnius or Kaunas.

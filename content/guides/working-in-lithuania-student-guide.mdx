## Legal Working Rights for International Students

Non-EU students enrolled in accredited Lithuanian universities or colleges holding a valid **Temporary Residence Permit (TRP)** have the right to work.

### Work Hour Allowances
- **Bachelor's Degree Students:** Up to 20 hours per week during the academic semester (unrestricted during official holidays and summer breaks).
- **Master's and PhD Students:** Unrestricted working hours (up to 40 hours per week) throughout their studies.

## What Documents Do You Need?

Before your employer can legally register you with **Sodra** (State Social Insurance), you must provide:
1. Valid Passport and biometric TRP card.
2. University Enrollment Certificate confirming active student status.
3. Lithuanian Bank Account (IBAN) for direct wage deposits.
4. Tax Identification Number (automatically assigned with your TRP registration via VMI).

## Where to Find Student-Friendly Jobs

- **CV-Online.lt & CVbankas.lt:** Search with filters set to English language.
- **MeetFrank:** Fast mobile applications for tech, operations, and startup roles.
- **Logistics & Warehousing:** Companies like DPD, Omniva, and local distribution centers often hire without requiring Lithuanian fluency.
- **Customer Support & IT:** International hubs in Vilnius and Kaunas frequently look for multilingual talent.

## Taxes and Sodra Deductions

In Lithuania, employment income is subject to:
- **Personal Income Tax (GPM):** Standard rate of 20% on gross salary.
- **Health Insurance (PSD) and Social Insurance (VSD):** Automatically deducted by your employer.
- **Annual Tax Return (GPM308):** As a student, you can often claim tax refunds between March and May each year via the Electronic Declaration System (EDS).

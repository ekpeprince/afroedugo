## Why You Need a Lithuanian or EU Bank Account

As an international student in Lithuania, having a local or SEPA-compliant European IBAN is essential for:
1. Receiving salary from part-time student employment.
2. Paying university tuition and dormitory fees with zero exchange fees.
3. Submitting proof of financial sufficiency for your TRP renewal.
4. Setting up local mobile plans (Telia, Bite, Tele2).

## Top Banking Options for International Students

### 1. Paysera (Easiest & Fastest for Non-EU Passports)
Paysera is a Lithuanian electronic money institution licensed by the Bank of Lithuania.
- **Requirements:** Passport and selfie verification via their mobile app.
- **Physical Card:** Visa card delivered to your Lithuanian address in 3-5 business days.
- **Best For:** Instant setup before receiving your physical TRP card.

### 2. Revolut
Lithuania is Revolut's European banking headquarters (Revolut Bank UAB).
- **Requirements:** Valid Lithuanian TRP (or national visa D) and passport.
- **Features:** Clean app, multi-currency pockets, and zero fee domestic transfers.

### 3. Traditional Banks (Swedbank & SEB)
- **Process:** Visit a local branch in Vilnius or Kaunas with your TRP, passport, and university acceptance letter.
- **Account Fee:** Free student banking plans are available for enrolled students under 26.

## University Dormitories vs. Private Apartments

Securing accommodation is one of the top priorities upon receiving your university acceptance letter in Lithuania.

### 1. University Dormitories
- **Average Cost:** €120 - €250 per month (utilities typically included).
- **Pros:** Cheaper, great way to meet fellow international students, close to campus.
- **Cons:** Shared kitchen and bathrooms, limited single rooms, early application deadlines.

### 2. Private Apartment Rentals
- **Average Cost:** €300 - €500+ per month plus utilities (€80 - €160 depending on winter heating).
- **Popular Neighborhoods in Vilnius:** Naujamiestis, Antakalnis, Šnipiškės, Senamiestis (Old Town).
- **Popular Neighborhoods in Kaunas:** Centras, Žaliakalnis, Dainava.

## Where to Look for Verified Rentals
- **Aruodas.lt:** The primary real estate portal in Lithuania. Filter by furnished (`įrengtas`) and central heating.
- **Facebook Groups:** Search for "Foreigners in Vilnius/Kaunas Flat Rent".
- **AfroEduGo Housing Board:** Community-shared verified student listings from African scholars abroad.

## Key Rules Before Signing a Lease
1. **Never send a deposit before inspecting the property or verifying ownership.**
2. **Declaration of Residence:** Ensure the landlord officially agrees to let you declare your place of residence (*gyvenamosios vietos deklaravimas*), which is strictly mandatory for your TRP card collection.

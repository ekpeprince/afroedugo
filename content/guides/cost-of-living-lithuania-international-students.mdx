## Why Lithuania is One of Europe's Most Affordable Student Hubs

Compared to Western European destinations like Germany, the Netherlands, or France where monthly living costs easily exceed €1,200 to €1,600, Lithuania offers high-caliber European living for a fraction of the cost.

With an average monthly expenditure of **€450 to €750**, students enjoy comfortable living, high-speed fiber internet, pristine green spaces, and safe urban environments.

## Accommodation Options: Dormitories vs. Private Rentals

### 1. University Dormitories (Best for Budget & Socializing)
Most major universities (e.g., Vilnius University, KTU, VMU, VILNIUS TECH, LSMU) provide on-campus student residences.
- **Cost**: €90 – €220 / month (utilities and high-speed Wi-Fi included).
- **Setup**: Usually 2 students sharing a twin room with shared kitchenette and private or shared bathroom.
- **Application Tip**: Dormitory allocations open early (May to July for Autumn semester). Apply as soon as your acceptance is confirmed.

### 2. Private Shared Apartments (Best for Independence)
Sharing a 2- or 3-bedroom apartment with other international students is common across Kaunas and Vilnius.
- **Cost per room**: €200 – €350 / month + utility bills (€50–€100/mo depending on winter heating).
- **Contracts**: Standard leases are 12 months with a 1- or 2-month security deposit.

## Student Discounts: The Power of the LSP Card

The **Lithuanian Student Identity Card (Lietuvos studento pažymėjimas - LSP)** is issued to all officially enrolled degree students in Lithuania. It unlocks massive statutory discounts:
- **80% off municipal public transit** (buses and trolleybuses).
- **50% off intercity trains and passenger buses** across Lithuania (e.g., Vilnius to Kaunas train for less than €4).
- Discounted gym memberships, museum admissions, and cinema tickets.
